Published December 2025

Energy Efficient Software Development for Mobile Applications

Adewumi, I.O., Arikeuyo, A.O., Amuda, H., Alao, K.O.

Journal of Chemical, Environmental and Biological Engineering · Vol. 9(2), pp. 83–94

An integrated framework for energy-efficient Android development combining static code analysis, dynamic energy profiling, and experimental testing across four apps — identifying anti-patterns like unreleased wake locks and excessive CPU activity, and achieving a 30–40% reduction in energy usage post-optimization.

Preprint August 2025

Software Patterns for the 21st Century: From Monolithic Designs to AI-Driven Architectures

Badru, R.A., Arikeuyo, A.O., Adewumi, I.O.

Research Square · Systematic Review

A PRISMA-guided systematic review of 42 peer-reviewed studies on software design patterns, mapping the shift from monolithic to distributed, event-driven, and cloud-native architectures, and surfacing recurring trade-offs across pattern categories.

Preprint September 2025

Comparative Analysis of Test Coverage Metrics in Agile vs. Traditional Software Development

Ajayi, W., Arikeuyo, A.O., Suleiman, A.O., Olorunnisola, A.E., Adewumi, I.O.

Research Square

A mixed-method study comparing statement, branch, and path coverage across 30 Agile vs. Traditional software projects, finding Agile achieves significantly higher and more stable coverage — with a Random Forest classifier distinguishing methodologies at 87% accuracy.

Adewumi, I.O., Oyekunle, V.B., Arikeuyo, A.O., Babatunde, A.A.

Research Square

A sequence-modeling neural network approach for predicting post-harvest losses in cassava-based fufu production across selected hubs in Oyo State, addressing the limits of manual spoilage inspection.
